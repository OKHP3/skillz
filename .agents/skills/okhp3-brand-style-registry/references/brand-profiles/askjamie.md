# AskJamie brand overlay

Use [askjamie.bot](https://askjamie.bot) as the primary public reference for this fork. AskJamie is a friendly AI helpdesk: a calm, plain-language thinking partner for decisions, tradeoffs, next steps, walkthroughs, and branching logic.

## Capture and application rules

- Record observed site evidence separately from inferred design interpretation and owner-declared rules.
- Prefer muted teals, paper-grain warmth, steady pacing, readable hierarchy, and diagrams when they genuinely clarify.
- Apply one primary AskJamie profile. Do not blend in Glee-fully sparkle or turn the helpdesk into a generic corporate dashboard.
- Explain decisions plainly; avoid jargon for its own sake. Preserve accessibility, contrast, responsive behavior, and owner approval.
- Use the profile for AskJamie surfaces and decision-support artifacts, not as a claim of official platform authority.

Use `askjamie` as the stable profile identifier unless the target repository has a stronger local convention.
