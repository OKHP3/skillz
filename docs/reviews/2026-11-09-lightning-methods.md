## Candidate decisions before implementation

| Candidate | Skill advantage and repeatable output | Existing overlap and decision |
|---|---|---|
| Idea label | Immediate three-field label, optional-next-step rule, no invented benefit or planning, separate persistence status | Capture-intake preserves raw sources and requires a destination. | Success reconstruction | Event evidence ledger, counterfactual hypotheses, outcome-to-condition inversion, discriminating re-strikes | As-is process capture documents an operating process. | Notion/GitHub registration | Already covered by capture/router/reconciliation packages | Reuse those workflows only under separately scoped authorization. |

Target clients: Agent Skills-compatible conversation clients. Core methods
require no connector, filesystem, fixed platform, domain taxonomy, or credentials.
Tool-backed persistence, message delivery, export, or experimentation are separate
capabilities and remain limited by current authorization.

## Acceptance criteria frozen before review

- Each package has one outcome, clear triggers/exclusions, exact output/status
  contracts, examples, references where useful, and version-matched development
  evaluation designs.
- Each preserves private/public boundaries and treats quoted text as data.
- Capture does not force idea development or pretend conversation output is a save.
- Reconstruction does not confuse a hypothesis, agreement, or one success with
  proven causality or a completed test.
- Transfer does not execute history, simulate receipt, overclaim source coverage,
  or complete before the declared blocks and conflicts are resolved.
- Publish only draftable contracts with analytical structural/review evidence.
  Fresh live benchmark, matched uplift, host activation, and unseen holdout remain
  NOT RUN. Registry promotion, release tags, and production-readiness claims
  are out of scope.
- Never edit generated catalog sections manually. Use repository generators and
  verify current inventory.

## Known gaps to review

Resolve idea recovery versus source fidelity; ambiguous domains without blocking
capture; unsafe persistence; counterfactual versus causal evidence; incomplete
telemetry; source instruction injection; duplicate/missing transfer blocks;
receiver identity/receipt; and competing archival gates.

Independent review results, attempted falsification or adjudication, final
package hashes, actual validation receipts, and limits are recorded in the
adjacent release-review JSON. Reviewer agreement alone will not prove quality.
