# LinkedIn series continuity: review

Decision: **approve-with-limits** for an analytical source candidate. This does
not establish host interoperability, task-quality uplift, or holdout success.

## Design and placement

The series skill composes existing angles, post and voice workflows, adding
prior-item identity and state, continuity checks, and a private evidence
handoff. An optional proof-brief workflow may supply a candidate packet; its
output remains untrusted and all claim and disclosure checks still apply.
The package is draft-only, and neutral examples avoid private source material.

A separate scheduled-post revision candidate was evaluated but is not included
here: an active LinkedIn publishing-operations proposal overlaps its scope.
That candidate remains preserved separately pending a deliberate placement
and interaction decision. Career-specific resume skills remain evidence
sources, not duplicates of this social-series contract.

## Equilibrium review

Three separately prompted evidence, outcome and safety reviewers shared the
inherited model family, conversation context and some sources; their agreement
is correlated. Initial findings identified a conflict with angle selection,
an ambiguous proof-packet fallback, and a risk that a prior post by a different
or unknown author could be misattributed. Each led to a contract correction and
synthetic development case.

After revised reviews materially agreed, the disruptor tested author and series
identity, untrusted proof-packet authority, and direct-draft routing. The author
identity objection survived the initial instructions and was corrected. A
bounded re-read found the revised contract addresses it. The negotiator accepted
the final frozen instruction hash with limits. The final capitalization and
line-wrap edit was confirmed to be nonsemantic. Attempted falsification is not
proof of reliable host behavior.

## Evaluation limits

The package has a neutral worked example and development-case design. One
same-model, author-visible synthetic exercise was run on an earlier candidate;
it is not a benchmark of this version. The final attribution and proof-packet
cases were walked through analytically, not run in a live host. No protected
holdout, independently graded matched baseline, or actual optional proof-brief
integration is recorded. Owner review and disclosure approval for any
generated post remain separate. Re-review after material instruction, host,
consent-policy or evidence changes.
