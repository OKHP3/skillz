# Series continuity and scheduled revision: review

Decision: **approve-with-limits** for an analytical, reviewable candidate.
This is not a claim of behavioral effectiveness, platform compatibility,
protected-holdout success, or measured skill uplift.

## Design and placement

The series skill composes the existing angles, post and voice skills, adding
verified prior-item states, source-backed continuity and a private evidence
handoff. The scheduled-post skill is a separate mechanics contract: it targets
one existing queue entry and verifies observable preservation and readback.
The text-only social family does not gain scheduling powers. Career-specific
resume and portfolio skills remain adjacent sources, not duplicated packages.

The intended inputs are user-selected prior posts, source evidence, voice and
authorization; the outputs are a reviewable draft or a bounded queue receipt.
Missing evidence, ambiguous identity, unobservable required assets, unsafe
timing, a conflicting writer or a missing host must stop the dependent action.
Public examples are synthetic; the owner and real sources are runtime inputs.

## Equilibrium review

Three separately prompted reviewers examined evidence, outcome and safety.
They shared the inherited model family, thread context and overlapping source
set, so their agreement is correlated. Initial findings required direct-draft
routing that respects the angles skill's owner-selection contract, a timing
check, visible formatting verification, and a pre-save concurrent-writer check.
The author corrected these; independent bounded re-reads found no remaining
material defect in those areas.

Once revised reviewers materially agreed, a disruptor proposed a deadline
crossing while the editor is open and the queue remains unchanged. The initial
revision checked time only before editing; the final candidate rechecks the
clock and host semantics immediately before save. The corresponding synthetic
development case expects a stop. A second case covers unobservable required
media. Analytical re-review found the written contracts address both. This is
an attempted falsification, not proof that any host executes them correctly.

The negotiator chose approve-with-limits based on the frozen instruction hashes
and the correction evidence. The machine-readable ledger records the role
outcomes, artifact hashes, constraints, follow-ups and review expiry.

## Evaluation and limits

Each package has a synthetic worked example and development-case design. Two
same-model, author-visible exercises produced a bounded series draft and a stop
on a simulated conflicting queue change; the scheduler exercise predates the
final deadline correction. They are informal development observations, not a
frozen matched benchmark or independent grading. The final deadline and media
cases were reviewed analytically; no live host execution, unseen holdout,
comparative uplift, or general effectiveness was established.

The fail-closed pre-save check may make some interfaces unusable. A non-atomic
recheck cannot eliminate a last-instant race. Draft text still requires owner
review; publication and scheduling are separate authorized host actions.
Re-review after a material skill, host, consent-policy or evidence change.
