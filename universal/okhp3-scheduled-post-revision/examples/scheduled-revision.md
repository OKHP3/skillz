# Synthetic scheduled revision

An owner identifies one queued item and separately has an open composer draft.
The owner authorizes replacing the queued text while retaining the observed
scheduled time and media. The operator verifies identity and settings, uses the
queue item's edit action, then reads the queue again. The receipt reports the
new text and retained observable settings as scheduled. A publication result is
unknown until independently observed. If the queue has two indistinguishable
items or a setting cannot be inspected, stop and report the ambiguity instead.
