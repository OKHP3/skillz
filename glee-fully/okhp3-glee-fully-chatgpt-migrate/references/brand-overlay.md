# Glee-fully ChatGPT migration overlay

Route reviewed, public-safe ChatGPT project material into the Glee-fully toolbox. Preserve the original project identity and provenance before translating it into Tool or Tool-ette records.

## Required interpretation

- Treat Glee-fully positioning as evidence of tone and purpose, not proof that every conversation is product canon.
- Preserve voice, personalizability, intended user, sibling relationships, and “best for” moments as labeled fields when present.
- Keep private family details, credentials, raw exports, and uncertain assistant claims in private staging or explicitly mark them unresolved.
- Use `glee-fully` routing only when the project’s evidence supports a Glee-fully destination; otherwise retain a neutral migration record.
- A migrated record must explain what was preserved, transformed, omitted, and still needs owner review.
