The [Texas forms index](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/forms/) lists notice-of-protest population variants 50-132 and 50-132-A, homestead application 50-114, and evidence affidavit 50-283. An affidavit's signing or notarization requirements require reading the actual applicable form. Availability in the index alone does not establish a filled form's validity. The skill should preserve field/page references, unresolved inputs, required owner actions, the district's supported filing route, and the edition/retrieval date.

The [Comptroller's protest guidance](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/protests/) supports a general notice/filing and informal-to-ARB workflow; the owner's actual notice governs case-specific planning. The [valuation guidance](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/valuing-property.php) distinguishes the homestead limitation on appraised value and new-improvement treatment. Savings must be calculated from the resulting taxable value for each taxing unit, not from a market-value reduction alone.
