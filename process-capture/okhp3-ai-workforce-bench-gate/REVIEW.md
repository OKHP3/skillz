The protocol required the same inherited model in separate contexts, identical synthetic input, with-skill access to the frozen instructions/reference, and baseline exclusion from those materials. A separate grader scored anonymized responses against six frozen expectations. The integrator confirmed **A = with-skill; B = without-skill** after blinded grading.

| Condition | Descriptive result |
|---|---|
| A, with skill | 6 of 6 expectations passed |
| B, without skill | 3 of 6 expectations passed |

A included an explicit material-learning gate, funded formation and observed-proficiency requirements, and fuller transition/talent cost accounting. B omitted material parts of those three criteria. Both preserved the authority, privacy and discrimination boundaries on response evidence. The grader did not independently audit actual tool actions.

**This is n=1, not a generalizable uplift result.** The fixture was integrator-authored and visible, not a protected holdout. No actual workforce pilot or human outcome was tested.
