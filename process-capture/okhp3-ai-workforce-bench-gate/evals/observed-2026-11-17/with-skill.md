## 6. Balanced economics and scenario model

**There is no defensible headcount or cash-savings estimate in the supplied material.** No extraction-speed value is provided, and a task-time improvement would still not establish that four entire roles are avoidable. Include remaining duties, peaks, exceptions, coverage, human validation and supervision in the workload model. [S1–S4]

For future modeling, define monthly released capacity as:

`released capacity hours = (monthly eligible messages × (baseline human minutes per message − future human minutes per message)) / 60 − additional non-case mentor/oversight hours`.

Future human minutes must include review, correction, exceptions and rework. Count each hour once. Measure separately whether released hours can be redeployed, reduce overtime or avoid planned hiring. Retained salary is not cash savings. Any eventual staffing consequence requires its own evidenced and authorized decision.

For each horizon `H` in 1, 3 and 5 years:

`net cash benefit(H) = verified cash costs avoided through H − transition cost − incremental recurring cost − incremental quality/customer losses − incremental mentor cost − incremental talent-replacement cost`.

Use consistent currency and price basis, document discounting if used, and avoid charging the same rework or mentor hour twice. Talent costs belong in sensitivity analysis until supported by data; they are not automatically zero or a known loss.
