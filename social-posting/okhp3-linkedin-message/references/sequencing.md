# Sequencing related messages

Use when two or more messages concern one subject, or when one message
mentions another recipient.

## Order rules

1. **Ally before target.** A close contact who can critique the approach goes
   first, so their feedback can change the main message before it is sent.
2. **Target before mutuals who may tell the target.** A recipient who knows
   the target should hear about the outreach after the target has received it,
   so the target hears it first-hand.
3. **Claims must be true on arrival.** If a message says "I am reaching out to
   `<RECIPIENT>`", it must be sent after that outreach, or worded as future
   intent.
4. **Ask for discretion only where it helps.** One plain sentence to the ally
   ("keep this between us until I've reached out") is enough. Never ask a
   recipient to conceal something from someone who has a right to know it.

## Cross-recipient leakage check

Read every draft as if it will be forwarded to every other recipient in the
sequence. Flag and offer an alternative for:

- urgency or financial pressure that would weaken the stance taken with the
  target;
- a strategy the ally may see but the target should not see described;
- a characterization of one recipient that another recipient would find
  unflattering.

## Output

Return a numbered send order with, for each step, the recipient, the
condition that must hold before it is sent (for example, "after the ally
replies" or "the day after the target message"), and the draft's sequence
dependency.
