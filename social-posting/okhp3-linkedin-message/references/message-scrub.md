# Private-message scrub

Run after drafting and before returning. A private message can be screenshot
and forwarded; write as if it will be. Flag each item and offer a replacement.
Do not silently delete content the user dictated.

- **Loaded or clinical language.** Clinical terms (for example, diagnoses used
  as metaphors), "toxic," "sabotage," or similar language reads differently
  out of context. Offer a plain-language alternative and let the user choose.
- **Urgency that can travel.** Statements about deadlines, finances, or
  pressure may be appropriate for one recipient and harmful if they reach
  another. Cross-check with `sequencing.md`.
- **Disparagement.** Remove or neutralize negative statements about a former
  or current employer, a competitor, or a named person.
- **Confidential detail.** Remove amounts, internal names, unannounced news,
  client or customer identities, and anything the user's agreements restrict.
- **Unsupported claims.** A metric, title, date, or outcome must come from the
  user or a cited source.
- **Contact details.** Include a phone number or email only when the user asks
  for it in that message.
- **Formatting.** Keep paragraphs short for mobile reading. Avoid formatting
  the surface does not render.
