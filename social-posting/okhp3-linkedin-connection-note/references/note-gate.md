# Connection Note Gate

Run this gate after the voice pass and before returning notes.

- Count characters for each note, including spaces and punctuation. Report the
  count. Reject any note over the limit minus the headroom.
- Trace every claim about the sender to the supplied fact sheet. Remove any
  credential, result, number, affiliation, or opinion that is not on it.
- Trace every claim about the recipient to supplied public context. Do not
  quote or paraphrase private information, inferred motives, or personal
  details beyond the professional hook.
- Remove employer-identifying language unless the sender explicitly approved it
  for public use. When a separation, non-solicit, or confidentiality agreement
  is in play, the sender must supply the rules. Do not use recruiting or
  poaching language toward people at a former employer, and do not describe the
  sender's employment status unless the sender supplied the wording.
- Remove any ask beyond connecting. No pitch, link drop, referral request, job
  request, or meeting request in the invite itself.
- Confirm uniqueness. No two notes share the same first six words or the same
  hook phrasing.
- Apply `okhp3-linkedin-voice`: natural contractions, no em dashes, no filler
  praise, no hashtags, no emoji unless the sender asked for one.
- Return the notes and the report only. Approval to send one batch does not
  authorize a later batch.
