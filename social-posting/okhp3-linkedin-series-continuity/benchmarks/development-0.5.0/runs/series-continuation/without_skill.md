In my last post, I shared how I was designing a reusable workflow. I’ve since committed the template, and finishing it changed how I think about what makes a workflow reusable.

When I’m doing something myself, I can fill in the gaps from memory. Writing the template made me ask which decisions I could make ahead of time and which ones should stay open for whoever uses it next.

That’s the distinction I’ll carry into the next workflow I design: make the starting point clear without pretending every run will look the same.
