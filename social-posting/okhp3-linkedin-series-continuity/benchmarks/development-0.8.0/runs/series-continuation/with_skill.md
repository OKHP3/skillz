## LinkedIn draft

In an earlier post, I shared how I was designing a reusable workflow. The template for that workflow is now committed.

That’s a good moment to ask a design question: What should the template decide for the next person, and what should it leave up to them?

A template can give someone a starting point without making every choice for them. The line between the two is worth thinking about before the next use.

When you turn a workflow into a template, what do you make a default, and what do you leave open?
