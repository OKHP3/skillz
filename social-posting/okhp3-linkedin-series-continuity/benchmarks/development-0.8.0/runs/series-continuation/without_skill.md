In my last post, I shared how I was designing a reusable workflow. Since then, I’ve committed the finished template to the repository.

Finishing it made me think about what I mean by “reusable.” A workflow can make perfect sense while I’m building it, when all the decisions are still fresh. The more interesting question is whether the template gives me enough to pick it up again once I’ve forgotten the details.

That’s what I’ll pay attention to the next time I use it. Where can I follow the template as written, and where do I still have to reconstruct a decision?
