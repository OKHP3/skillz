In my last post, I shared how I was designing a reusable workflow. Since then, I’ve committed the template to the repository.

The shift from design to template gave me a different question to think about. When I’m designing a workflow, I’m asking, “How should this work?” When I’m turning it into a template, I’m asking, “What would I need in front of me to do this again?”

That second question is the one I want to carry into future builds. It gives me a concrete way to think about reuse while I’m still close enough to the work to remember the decisions behind it.
