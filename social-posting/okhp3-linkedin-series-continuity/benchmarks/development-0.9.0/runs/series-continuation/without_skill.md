In my last post, I wrote about designing a workflow I could reuse. Since then, I’ve committed the template to the repository.

The part I keep coming back to is how the question changed. When I was designing the workflow, I was asking, “What needs to happen, and in what order?” Turning it into a template raised a different question: “What would I need written down if I came back to this in a month?”

I like that second question because it makes “reusable” concrete. It asks me to think about what I’d otherwise have to remember or figure out again. The template is now there to try the next time the work comes up.
