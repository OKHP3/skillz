When you test a new workflow, what would convince you it made a difference?

Start by giving the baseline a fair shot at the same task. Then choose checks that can distinguish the two approaches on something that matters, such as how often a person has to step in or how well each handles an ambiguous input.

A passing check tells you the workflow met the bar you set. To decide whether to adopt it, you also need to know how much the workflow helped clear that bar.
