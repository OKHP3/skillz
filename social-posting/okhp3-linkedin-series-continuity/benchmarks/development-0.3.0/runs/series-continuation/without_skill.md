In my last post, I shared how I was designing a reusable workflow. Since then, I’ve finished the workflow and committed the template to the repository.

Writing the template made me think differently about what it means for a workflow to be reusable. When I’m the one doing the work, it’s easy to carry the reasoning in my head. A template makes me decide what needs to be written down and where the next person should have room to make their own call.

That’s the question I’m taking into the next workflow I design: what would someone need to know if I weren’t there to walk them through it?
