In my last post, I wrote about designing a reusable workflow. Since then, I’ve committed the template to the repository.

The part I keep coming back to is what changes when you turn a workflow into a template. When you’re describing a process, it’s easy to gloss over a decision you already know how to make. A template asks you to spell out which steps should carry over and where the next person needs room to use their judgment.

That’s the question I’ll bring to the next workflow I design: what can I make easier to repeat, and what should I leave open?
