In my last post, I shared how I was designing a reusable workflow. Since then, I’ve committed the template to the repository.

The shift from design to template made me think differently about what “done” means. When I’m designing a workflow, I can fill in the gaps from memory. Writing it down for reuse asks a different question: what context would I need if I came back to this later?

That’s what I’m taking into the next workflow I design. I want to ask that question while the decisions are still fresh.
