In my last post, I wrote about designing a checklist. The checklist is now complete and ready to reuse.

Designing it meant deciding what belonged on the list. Making it reusable meant asking a different question: would each step still make sense the next time around, without the context I had when I wrote it?

That’s the distinction I’m taking forward. A checklist can capture what to do today. A reusable one should also make it clear where to start next time.
