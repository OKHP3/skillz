In my last post, I shared that I was designing a reusable checklist. I’ve finished it, and it’s ready for the next time I need to run through the process.

The idea was simple. If I’m going to do something again, I want to give myself a place to start. A checklist lets me come back to the steps I’ve already thought through instead of reconstructing them from memory.

The next time I use it, I’ll pay attention to where I still have to stop and figure out what to do. Those are the places I’ll revisit.
