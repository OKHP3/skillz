# Expanded public-claim evaluation: release decision

Decision: **defer for evidence**. This candidate is not approved for catalog
promotion. Its synthetic development runs do not establish broad reliability.

The version-specific 14-case rubric recorded 56/56 with the skill and 41/56
without it, one fresh read-only, no-tool session per case and arm. The frozen
protocol and its five input hashes were committed before execution. The rubric
and fixtures were author-visible; grading used separately prompted sessions
from the same model family with labels hidden but potentially inferable.

A separate safety review found that `direct-draft-routing` put an unsupported
first-person recommendation in the public draft. The grade for its all-claims
criterion quoted only the supported opening sentences. Outcome and evidence
reviewers considered that wording borderline but also flagged that the cited
excerpt omitted it. The negotiation decision is to keep the gate blocked: an
unverified owner preference conflicts with the skill's explicit contract even
when it is a rhetorical recommendation. The recorded scores remain unchanged
as historical grader output, with this independent finding recorded separately.

The next release candidate needs stronger removal of unsupported subjective
first-person language and a full-response audit that does not rely on one
selected excerpt. It also needs an independently protected unseen evaluation,
real host integration checks, and a separate release review before broader
claims. The public Forge catalog must not be rebuilt or promoted on this run.
