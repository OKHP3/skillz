In my last post, I shared how I was designing a reusable workflow. I’ve since committed the template to the repository.

Looking back, the part I keep thinking about is what it means to make a workflow reusable. A design can make perfect sense while all the decisions are still fresh in your head. A template has to carry enough of that context for someone to pick it up later, even if that someone is you.

That’s the question I’ll bring to the next workflow I design: what would I need to know to start from this template a month from now?
