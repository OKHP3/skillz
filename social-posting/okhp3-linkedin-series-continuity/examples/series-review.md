# Synthetic continuity example

The owner supplies a verified published item about an earlier idea and a private
test report for a new prototype. The report describes fictional inputs and
shows equal treatment and baseline results. A second prior item is scheduled,
with no publication receipt. The owner requests a draft for review.

The series record marks the first item published, the second scheduled, the
new test private and fictional, and observed uplift as none. The proposed
continuation discusses the lesson without disclosing the private source or
claiming the scheduled item appeared. It routes through post drafting, voice,
and the final public-context scrub. Owner review remains pending.
