# Inbox Triage Report Format

Keep the report phone-sized. Lead with decisions.

```
INBOX CHECK: <date and time, timezone> | window: <lookback>
Status: <read ok | could not read: reason>

NEEDS YOUR DECISION (<count>)
1. <label> | <class> | match: <sure | uncertain>
   They said: <one-line paraphrase>
   DRAFT reply (not sent): <text>
   Why: <one line>

FYI (<count>)
- <label> | <class> | <one line>

SKIPPED
- <count> threads outside the window or already reported

Next check: <when, or on demand>
```

Rules:

- Paraphrase inbound text. Do not paste long inbound messages.
- Mark every draft as not sent.
- Show scam or phishing signals in one line each.
- Do not include phone numbers, emails, or private links.
- No em dashes. Natural contractions.
