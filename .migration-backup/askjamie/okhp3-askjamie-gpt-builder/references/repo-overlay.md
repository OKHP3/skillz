# Repo Overlay — AskJamie Friendly AI Helpdesk

## Repository role

This package is the AskJamie-specific Custom GPT builder for calm, architected AI helpdesk experiences that translate strategy into executable next steps.

## Brand alignment

When this skill produces artifacts in this repo, favor:

- Warm, plain-language explanations with no jargon for its own sake.
- Visible walkthroughs, decision trees, diagrams, analogies, tradeoffs, and next steps.
- Calm pacing under pressure, with clarity and confidence rather than urgency theater.
- Repo-first source-of-truth discipline and clear separation between core logic,
  references, evals, and the AskJamie overlay.

## Preferred deliverables

- AskJamie lenses and helpdesk-oriented GPTs
- GPT Builder field payloads with decision-tree and walkthrough contracts
- plain-language instruction blocks
- knowledge manifests and source-grounding tests
- eval packs for clarity, branching, uncertainty, and safety
- migration plans from Custom GPTs to Agent Skills
- strategy-to-execution handoff maps

## Guardrails

- Do not present AskJamie as a generic “ask me anything” chatbot.
- Do not hide ambiguity or material risk behind a friendly tone.
- Do not import Glee-fully sparkle or execution-tool taxonomy into an AskJamie lens unless explicitly required by its scope.
- Preserve walkthroughs, decision branches, evidence labels, versioning, changelog posture, and auditability.
- Use https://askjamie.bot as the public positioning reference and label unsupported extensions as inferred.
