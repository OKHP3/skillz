# NFL fantasy glossary

| Term or abbreviation | Plain-language meaning | Calculation role |
|---|---|---|
| ADP, Average Draft Position | Typical draft position in a league or market | Describes cost or market expectation |
| DFS, Daily Fantasy Sports | Fantasy contests based on a short slate | Usually adds salary, contest, and payout constraints |
| PPR, Points Per Reception | Scoring one point or another value for a reception | Changes player projection weights |
| Snap share | Share of team plays on which a player participates | Proxy for opportunity |
| Target share | Share of team pass targets sent to a player | Receiver opportunity measure |
| Route participation | Share of eligible pass routes run | Separates playing time from raw targets |
| Floor | A lower plausible performance range | Used for risk-aware decisions, not a guarantee |
| Ceiling | An upper plausible performance range | Used for tournament or upside decisions |
| Replacement value | Production available from the best realistic alternative | Converts raw projection into scarcity-aware value |
| Points per dollar | Projected points divided by salary | A cost-efficiency measure, not a complete lineup objective |
| Flex | A roster slot accepting players from multiple positions | Affects feasible lineups and replacement baselines |
| Correlation | Tendency for player outcomes to move together | Affects lineup portfolio risk and upside |
| Leverage | Difference between a player's usefulness and expected ownership | Relevant only when contest context and ownership data exist |

Always record scoring rules, roster rules, salary, slate, and decision date.
