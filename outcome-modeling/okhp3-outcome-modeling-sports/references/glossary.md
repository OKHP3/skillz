# Sports glossary

| Term or abbreviation | Plain-language meaning | Calculation role |
|---|---|---|
| EPA, Expected Points Added | Change in expected scoring value caused by a play | Measures play efficiency relative to the prior state |
| Elo rating | A rating updated from results against opponents | Provides a simple strength baseline |
| SOS, Strength of Schedule | Difficulty of the opponents faced | Prevents raw records from being compared without context |
| Opponent adjustment | Correction for the quality of opponents | Separates team performance from schedule difficulty |
| Home advantage | Average performance difference associated with venue | A matchup feature, not a guarantee |
| Regime change | A structural event such as a coaching, roster, or rule change | Limits how much older data should influence the state |
| Rest differential | Difference in recovery time between opponents | A context feature when supported by evidence |
| Snap share | Percentage of team plays on which a player participates | Measures opportunity in football models |
| Usage | Opportunities such as carries, targets, possessions, or minutes | Often predicts future production better than raw totals |
| Margin | Difference between competitors' scores or results | A richer signal than win/loss alone in many sports |
| Shrinkage | Pulling noisy estimates toward a prior or group average | Reduces overreaction to small samples |
| Calibration | Whether forecast probabilities match actual frequencies | Tests probability reliability |

Definitions vary by sport and data provider. Record the source definition before
combining metrics with the same name.
