# Sales glossary

| Term or abbreviation | Plain-language meaning | Calculation role |
|---|---|---|
| ACV, Annual Contract Value | Contract value expected in one year | Normalizes deal size |
| ARR, Annual Recurring Revenue | Recurring revenue expected per year | Measures subscription scale |
| ASP, Average Selling Price | Average price per sale | Helps compare deal economics |
| CAC, Customer Acquisition Cost | Cost to acquire a customer | Reduces expected contribution |
| Close rate / win rate | Share of opportunities that become wins | A raw outcome that requires opportunity context |
| Conversion rate | Share moving from one defined stage to another | Used in stage forecasts |
| Gross margin | Revenue left after direct delivery costs | Converts revenue into contribution potential |
| LTV, Lifetime Value | Expected economic value over a customer relationship | Includes retention and expansion assumptions |
| Pipeline coverage | Pipeline value divided by quota or target | Describes potential volume, not guaranteed revenue |
| Quota | Assigned sales target for a period | A constraint or performance reference |
| Retention | Customers or revenue remaining over time | Tests whether a win is durable |
| Expansion | Additional value from an existing customer | Adds future option value |
| Selection bias | Distortion from non-random opportunity assignment | A required fairness and validity check |
| Incremental lift | Difference from a comparable baseline action | Estimates potential action value |

Define whether revenue, margin, cost, and retention are booked, forecast, or
realized. Do not rank a person or territory on one metric without assignment and
opportunity-quality context.
