# Public work as interview evidence

Load this reference when a person wants to connect existing public writing,
code, models or prototypes to a target role, or considers publishing before an
interview. It selects evidence and prepares a drafting handoff; it does not
write or publish a social post.

## Inventory before narrative

For each artifact, record the supplied or verified public URL, author or exact
contribution, available date/version, status, supported role requirement,
disclosure scope and limits. Treat a supplied public URL as unverified until
its content has been read or a dated supplied capture is available. A source
that cannot be retrieved remains a coverage gap, not proof the artifact does
not exist. Do not inspect private accounts or internal systems to fill it.

Separate these signals:

| Artifact signal | Supports | Does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Delivered-work account | The specific supported action and outcome | Ownership of an entire program or undocumented metrics |
| Process or architecture method | Reasoning, structure and communication | Adoption, efficiency gains or universal applicability |
| Independent prototype | A hypothesis made concrete and bounded | Employer approval, production use, safety or market results |
| Publication date and visibility | A dated record and accessibility | Legal originality, influence on readers, or a hiring outcome |

Choose a role-relevant artifact because it helps answer a business question.
Explain the connection in one sentence, then name the evidential limit. An
artifact may show several signals, but its strongest claim still needs a source.
Corroborate personal contribution separately from project existence.

## Preserve the person's purpose

Public work may make longstanding expertise visible outside the person's
immediate working circle. Describe the expertise and the resulting artifact;
do not invent motives for prior leaders, imply they deliberately obstructed the
person, or frame publication as evading authority. Do not reduce the purpose to
confidence building unless the person says that is their purpose.

Maintain collaborator attribution. Acknowledgment of earlier work or syntax
does not imply inclusion in an upstream product or adoption as a standard.
Present collaboration as supported by the record, without territorial or
priority claims the sources cannot establish.

## Optional drafting handoff

If the user asks for a public draft, pass the chosen insight, public-safe proof,
source limits, audience, platform, desired voice and primary link to the
available drafting workflow. Employer identifiers or private details from an
interview packet do not become safe for a public post merely because this
reference was used. A draft can be generalized when disclosure is unsupported.

If timing is requested, confirm the actual event date, timezone and current
clock. Give a proposed window only. Do not imply the post was scheduled,
published, read by the interviewer, or responsible for an outcome. A past
interview cannot be treated as upcoming because an old note says so.
