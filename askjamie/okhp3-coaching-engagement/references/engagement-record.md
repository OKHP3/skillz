# Career coaching engagement record

Keep only information needed for the requested purpose. Runtime account, folder,
provider and candidate identities do not belong in the public skill package.

| Section | Required distinctions |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | engagement_id, candidate, provider, sponsor, engagement/application owners, actual host/project placement |
| Sources | exact source ID/link, source date, observed_at, authority and coverage limits |
| Service terms | written candidate-specific terms, oral statements, marketing, unknowns; start trigger versus date; coaching versus portal end |
| Privacy | ownership, processing, sponsor reporting, coach notes, file access, AI, subprocessors, retention/export/deletion, unresolved exceptions |
| Intake fields | source basis or owner choice, entered/saved/submitted state, readback evidence, blocked or declined fields |
| Documents | file/version identity, audience, recipient, authorized action, saved/uploaded/sent/acknowledged state |
| Appointments | exact date, local IANA timezone, UTC offset, reservation versus actual attendance, source invitation |
| Meeting closeout | decided, proposed, provider-asserted, owner-reported, unresolved; each action's owner, due date and passage |
| Plan | bounded outcomes, dates/quotas labeled agreed or proposed, next action and stopping condition |

Synthetic summary/transcript conflict:

- Summary: Sponsor sees no information; coach will rewrite three masters.
- Transcript: Sponsor receives start/completion status; coach says one combined
  foundational resume is the service, with three masters as inputs.
- Correct recap: attribute the confidentiality assurance and start/completion
  reporting, record one output estimate, leave full reporting scope unresolved.
  Do not create a completed action to send three new versions.

Synthetic portal gate:

- User has declined prior-salary disclosure. The portal offers only salary bands.
- Known contact fields may be entered or saved when authorized. If the required
  salary field prevents saving, leave it unanswered and report the exact gate.
  A provider-supported alternative can be requested in a draft. Do not guess,
  change hidden state, accept terms or send the request by implication.

An oral pause offer is not a confirmed extension. Record a requested pause,
provider acceptance and recalculated end date only from actual evidence.
A follow-up at 1 PM local across a daylight-saving transition needs the new
date's offset, not the prior meeting's offset.
