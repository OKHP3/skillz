# Lifetime career view contract

Consume existing career canon records rather than starting a second ledger.
The AskJamie canon uses stable claim IDs, assertions, effective/record dates,
use restrictions and supported, owner-attested, conflicting or unknown status.
Preserve the supplied schema or explicitly map equivalent fields without
silently upgrading their meaning. Supported reporting is not direct execution.

Minimum view manifest:

- view_id, subject_id, edition, as_of, temporal_mode and source_canon_edition;
- selected_claim_ids and source references with exact safe locators;
- included sections and any optional/private-context sections;
- selected/available/read source coverage with denominators and gaps;
- unresolved conflicts, exclusions and proposed_upstream_corrections;
- audience and owner_review status;
- produced formats, text/layout checks and actual saved identity/readback state.

Useful sections are biography/chronology, role and project registers, skills,
education and learning, a source-linked story/claim index, reflections and a
bibliography. Choose sections from the owner's purpose and evidence, not a
mandatory table of contents. Keep personal reflections visibly separate from
verified outcomes. A reference can preserve uncertain recollections without
presenting them as resume-ready facts.

Synthetic example: C1 and C2 describe the same period at one employer; do not
count them as two distinct jobs. C3 says coursework with degree conferral
unresolved; retain that gap. Twelve learning records cover nine unique titles
with no completion proof. Report records and titles, not twelve completed
courses. A generated biography citing C3 does not become another source for it.

A historical-cutoff view excludes later-available corrections unless the owner
requested a retrospective reconstruction. Keep correction date and effective
period visible, and send any canon dispute upstream. A full life history can
include early unrelated work as context without using it to imply current
qualification for a target role.

Use ordinary source links, never authentication-bearing or signed download
URLs. Shared extracts should omit private paths and contacts even when the
full authorized private reference retains safe internal source IDs.
