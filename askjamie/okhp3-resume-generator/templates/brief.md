# Candidate brief

Fill what you have. A pasted posting and rough work-history notes are enough to start. Leave optional fields empty.

- **Opening** · Employer, exact title and full posting. Paste the description if an external link requires sign-in.
- **Record** · Official job titles, employers, dates, and the current draft if you have one.
- **One story worth remembering** · What was at stake? What did you personally decide or deliver? What changed, for whom, and how do you know?
- **Working relationships** · Who reported to you, who worked alongside you, and who approved the decision or spending?
- **Supporting material** · Additional projects, interview notes or relevant work samples you can share. Identify estimates, unresolved dates, and restricted details.
- **Output preferences** · Requested length, file formats, contact fields, links, confidential names to omit, and any application instructions.
