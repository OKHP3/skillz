# Questions that bring out the evidence

If the user gives you a draft and a posting, begin there. Pick the smallest question that would improve a consequential line. Echo what you already know so the candidate can correct you quickly. Treat “I don’t know” as a useful answer. A concrete example often works without a number.

| When the record is thin | Ask in the candidate’s language |
|---|---|
| No target or record | “Could you paste the posting and your current résumé or work-history notes? Rough bullets are fine.” If you have one, ask only for the other. |
| An accomplishment hides behind “responsible for” | “Think of one time that went wrong. What did you notice, what did you change, and what did the next person do differently?” |
| The strongest example may be old | “When were you closest to the work this job owns? What did you handle then that your latest title might hide?” |
| “I led the team” | “Who reported to you day to day? Who had a different manager but relied on your direction? What decision did you need their agreement on?” |
| “I managed the budget” | “Which spending decision could you approve yourself? Did you own a budget, recommend an allocation, or track spend for someone else?” |
| Different groups disagreed | “What did each side need, where did they disagree, and what did they agree to do after your involvement?” |
| A number sounds promising | “What was being counted, over what period, and compared to what? If the number is uncertain, what did you deliver that you can describe confidently?” |
| A new tool or project is prominent | “Who used it, where, and for how long? What did you observe directly, and which benefits were estimates?” |
| A detail may be confidential | “Could we describe the type of customer, the decision and the result without naming the customer or sharing their records?” |
| Two sources conflict | “One version gives [A] and another gives [B]. Which can you stand behind? I can use the overlap or leave out the disputed detail.” |

**A quick start for the user**

> Tailor my résumé for the opening below. Here is the posting, the latest draft or notes I trust, and any length, format, contact or confidentiality preferences. Draw out whatever would materially change the hiring case.

**A revision prompt**

> Keep the parts of this draft that already work. The missing detail is [decision, leadership scope, or outcome]. I personally [action], the team [contribution], and we know [result] because [basis]. Show that where it has the most weight.

**A prompt for an uncertain metric**

> I remember the work better than the exact number. Ask me one question that could establish the outcome. If I can’t verify it, use an accurate qualitative result.
