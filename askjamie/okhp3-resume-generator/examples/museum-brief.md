# Worked example brief

This case is entirely fictional. The candidate uses an invented display name and a reserved example email address. The hiring museum, prior employers, school, posting, and evidence were invented for this exercise.

## Opening

**Collections Digitization Technician, Fen Lantern Museum**

A small museum is preparing a searchable collection of historical trade cards. The technician will scan fragile paper originals, match image files to accession records, and check images before staff release them to researchers. The museum requires hands-on experience digitizing paper collections and maintaining object records. Experience with EMu collections software is preferred.
